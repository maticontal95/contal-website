We are driven by a commitment to the built environment spanning multiple generations:

Born into a family of architects, Mati is trained as an architect, furniture-maker, and construction carpenter. Our general contracting practice is thus uniquely focused on quality and fidelity to designers' intentions.

✷

We maintain a continuing education in both contemporary building science and traditional building crafts. We're thus especially motivated to take on projects demanding handcrafted, vernacular materials and details (plaster mouldings, lime rendering, figured brickwork, carved elements, decorative parquet or mosaic work, etc.)

Our ambition is to work towards inscribing such techniques within a forward-looking, progressive design synthesis.
